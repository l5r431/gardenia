Clubs and activities

  • Mini football

    Friendly games for Years 1 to 6.

  • Gymnastics

    Rolls, balance and cartwheels.

  • Swimming club

    Fun in the school pool.

  • Dance

    Move to music from around the world.

  • Choir

    Two choirs, with concerts each term.

  • Drama

    A show every year, even for the youngest.

  • Messy art

    Paint, clay and collage.

  • Recorder and ukulele

    Learn a first instrument.

  • Forest club

    Fire circle, whittling and den building.

  • Gardening

    Grow vegetables for the school kitchen.

  • Bug hunters

    Magnifiers, nets and pond dipping.

  • Beach explorers

    Rock pools and shell sorting.

  • Reading buddies

    Older children read to younger ones.

  • Eco team

    Recycling, composting and litter picks.

  • School council

    Children bring ideas to the head.

  • Chess and games

    Think ahead, play fair, shake hands.

Forest school and the farm

Every class spends time in the woods and with our animals each week.

  • Wellies and waterproofs

    We go out in rain, frost and sun with the right kit.

  • Tools and fire

    Older children learn to use tools and a fire circle safely, with trained adults.

  • Animal care

    Chickens, goats and rabbits teach responsibility and gentleness.

Trips, year by year

  1. Reception

    Visit to the local farm and a teddy bears' picnic.

  2. Year 2

    Lighthouse visit and a sleepover in the school hall.

  3. Year 4

    Castle day and a museum workshop.

  4. Year 6

    Three-day residential with kayaking, camping and campfire songs.
