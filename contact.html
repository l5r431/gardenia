What happens on a visit

  1. A warm welcome

    You and your child are greeted at the door and shown around the classrooms.

  2. See learning happen

    Watch a lesson, pop into the woodland classroom and meet the farm animals.

  3. Chat with the Head

    Ask anything over a drink while your child plays with a buddy.

  4. Take your time

    Leave with a prospectus and no pressure. Come back as often as you like.

Getting here

  • Walking

    A walking bus leaves the harbour car park at 8:10 each morning, led by parent volunteers.

  • School bus

    Three routes cover the villages around Harbourside. Seats are booked termly.

  • By car

    Drop-off bays on Gardenia Lane are open from 8:15. Please do not park on the cliff road.

Common questions

Can my child come for a taster morning?

Yes. Taster mornings run most Wednesdays. Tell us the age and we will pair your child with a buddy.

How soon will I hear back?

We reply to visit requests within two working days.

Can siblings visit too?

Of course. Please mention their ages so we can plan.

Do you cater for allergies?

Yes. Our catering team follows each child's plan, and the kitchen is nut aware.

Where can I find policies?

Policies are shared with families on registration and in the parent portal.
