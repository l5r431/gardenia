How to apply

  1. Visit us

    Come to an open morning or book a private tour with your child.

  2. Register

    Fill in the form online. We ask about your child's interests, not for exam marks.

  3. Play morning

    Your child spends a relaxed morning in class while we chat with you.

  4. Offer

    We reply within two weeks and agree a settling-in plan together.

Settling in, gently

  • Taster sessions

    Short visits with a parent or carer nearby before the first full day.

  • A key person

    One adult is responsible for noticing how your child is feeling.

  • Daily photos

    Our youngest children's parents get photo updates in the first weeks.
