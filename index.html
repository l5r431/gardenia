At Gardenia every child is known by name, heard when they speak and trusted to try. We teach reading, number and kindness together, outside as often as we can, so that children arrive curious and leave confident.

A day at Gardenia

7:30

Breakfast club

Toast, quiet games and a gentle start for early arrivals.

8:45

Hello circle

We sing hello, see who is in today and share one piece of news each.

9:15

Sounds and stories

Letters, sounds and picture books on the story carpet.

10:30

Snack and outdoor play

Fruit first, then the mud kitchen, climbing frames and puddles.

11:15

Counting and making

Maths with shells, blocks and measuring jugs, not just worksheets.

12:15

Lunch together

Hot meals cooked on site, with vegetables from our own garden.

13:30

Forest or music

Woodland on Wednesdays, singing on Fridays and art in between.

15:15

Home time

A story, a tidy up and a wave goodbye. After-school club runs until 18:00.

    Six things we practise every day

    • Be curious

      Ask "why?" and then go and find out.

    • Be brave

      Try the hard thing. Mistakes are how we learn.

    • Be kind

      Notice who needs a friend and be one.

    • Be creative

      Paint, build, sing and invent without a fixed answer.

    • Be helpful

      Look after your class, your garden and each other.

    • Be proud

      Finish things, share them and say well done to others.
