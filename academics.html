    How reading grows

    Most children follow this path. Some move faster, some need longer, and we adjust for each one.

    1. Listen and talk

      Songs, rhymes and stories build vocabulary before children see a single letter.

    2. Letters and sounds

      Short daily phonics sessions teach the sounds and the letters that make them.

    3. Blend and read

      Children blend sounds into words and read books matched to what they know.

    4. Read to learn

      From Year 2 children read for meaning, discuss books and write about them.

    Support for every learner

    Some children need a little extra help, and some need extra challenge. Our learning support team works in class and in small groups, and teachers plan for both.

    • Early check-ins on speech, language and reading
    • Small-group catch-up with a trained adult
    • Enrichment for children who race ahead
    • Close work with parents and outside specialists

    How we share progress

    • Every day

      Teachers talk with children and note what they can now do.

    • Every term

      A short report, a parent meeting and next steps for home.

    • Once a year

      A full report. Years 2 and 6 also take light, friendly checks.

    Homework stays small: reading and a little practice, never long evenings.
